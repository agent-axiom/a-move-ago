# A MOVE AGO Telegram Bot Logo Design

## Goal

Create a square logo for the A MOVE AGO Telegram bot that communicates the
game's core mechanic: reconstructing the chess position immediately before the
last move. The mark should feel intellectual and mysterious while remaining
clear at Telegram avatar sizes.

## Approved direction

Use the **Reverse Move** concept selected from three alternatives. A large
ivory chess knight sits inside a copper-gold counterclockwise arrow. The arrow
forms a nearly closed circular ring around the knight, fully enclosing the
piece rather than appearing beside, behind, or across it.

## Composition

- Square 1:1 canvas suitable for a Telegram profile image.
- One centered chess knight with a bold, recognizable silhouette.
- One counterclockwise arrow forming a near-complete circular ring around the
  knight.
- All essential geometry stays within the central safe area so Telegram's
  circular crop does not cut off the knight or arrowhead.
- No typography, letters, board coordinates, or additional chess pieces.

## Visual language

- Mood: intellectual, mysterious, restrained, and contemporary.
- Style: polished, vector-friendly logo mark with strong flat shapes and only
  subtle depth in the background.
- Background: deep graphite-black, aligned with the application's `#151718`
  base color.
- Knight: warm ivory, aligned with the application's `#f5f2ea` foreground.
- Arrow: copper-gold, harmonizing with the application's warm wood chessboard
  palette.
- Contrast must remain strong when the logo is reduced to approximately 40 px.

## Constraints

- The arrow must clearly surround the knight as a ring.
- The arrow must point counterclockwise to communicate reversal.
- Avoid photorealism, glossy 3D rendering, ornate heraldry, visual clutter,
  thin lines, tiny details, text, signatures, borders, and watermarks.
- Do not make the arrow resemble a conventional clockwise refresh icon.

## Deliverable and validation

Generate one high-resolution square PNG. Validate the full-size result and a
small circular-crop preview for silhouette clarity, contrast, arrow direction,
and the required knight-inside-arrow relationship. Save the approved asset in
the project without replacing an existing file.

## Generation brief

Use case: `logo-brand`. Asset type: Telegram bot avatar. Create a centered,
vector-friendly logo mark on a deep graphite-black square background. Place a
bold warm-ivory chess knight inside a thick copper-gold counterclockwise arrow
that forms a nearly closed circular ring around the piece. Keep both elements
inside the central circular safe area. The result should feel intellectual,
mysterious, minimal, contemporary, and highly legible at 40 px. No text,
letters, extra pieces, board coordinates, ornate decoration, photorealism,
glossy 3D effects, thin details, border, signature, or watermark.
